01 — Introduction

About
the fig.

Ficus carica is a tree from the mulberry family. People have been growing it for a very long time. 1

02 — About the fig

What's
inside a fig?

A fig has many tiny flowers inside it. This is what makes it different from most other fruits.

Some figs need a small wasp to help them grow. Fig trees grow best in warm and dry weather. 2

03 — Figs in Azerbaijan

Figs in
Azerbaijan.

Verified

Azerbaijan's National Genebank is in Baku. It is part of the Genetic Resources Institute of ANAS. 3 4

There is no confirmed information here about specific fig varieties or where they are grown in Azerbaijan.

04 — Problems facing figs

What puts
figs at risk?

Drought and very hot weather make it harder for fig trees to grow.

New buildings, roads and pollution from the oil industry can damage places where fig trees grow.

When there are fewer farmers, old farming skills and traditional recipes can be forgotten.

These problems can affect fig trees and local traditions.

05 — Protecting figs

How can we
protect them?

We can protect fig trees, learn about different varieties and keep old farming knowledge alive. 3

Protect the figs.
Protect the future.

Figs are part of nature and local traditions. We should protect them for the future.
